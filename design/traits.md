# Traits (design; being built in waves)

A trait is something a companion is, not something they learn: it is rolled when they join and never changes. Every number below is a first guess to be tuned with the 200-run check.

## How they are handed out

- Every companion has 1 to 3 traits: 1 trait 75%, 2 traits 22%, 3 traits 3%.
- Rolled when the companion first appears: the two starters when the run begins, a tavern hire when the week's offers are rolled. The hire's paper shows them before you pay, so two offers of the same companion in different runs are different people.
- No trait appears twice in a run. A trait shown on a hire nobody took goes back into the pool when the week ends.
- Each trait has a rarity: common (weight 10), uncommon (5), rare (1). A companion's second and third traits are drawn the same way.
- A trait tied to a weapon or a school of magic goes to someone who can already use it 4 times in 5. The fifth time it lands on anyone: an axe trait on a swordsman is a reason to send them to the drill yard.
- A companion never gets two traits from the same line of a family (not both Sword: Sure and Axe: Sure) and never two two-edged traits.
- There are no purely bad traits. Two-edged ones give more than a plain trait and take something away.

Words used below: **Hit** and **Crit** are percentage points on the chance to hit or to strike critically, not points of a stat (for scale: 1 Dexterity is 5 Hit, 1 Critical is 2 Crit); **damage** is added to each blow. All three are the companion's own. **Avoid +10** means enemies have 10 less Hit against them. **Drawn more / less** changes how often enemies aim at them. Places in the line are counted as in the barracks: rear to front.

## Count

25 + 8 + 11 + 22 + 13 + 18 + 12 + 19 + 14 + 10 = **152 traits**. A full run sees perhaps 10 to 14 of them.

## Suggested order of building

1. **Wave 1 (about 95):** everything that is a number with a condition: families 1 to 5, the plain damage and Hit lines of family 6, family 8's growth traits. One data class (`TraitData`: a list of modifiers, each with an optional condition such as weapon type, place in the line, enemy trait, Health share, action count) covers them all, so adding more later is writing data, not code.
2. **Wave 2 (about 35):** hooks into single moments: on a kill, first blow, once per battle, day's end, the Infirmary, gold and materials.
3. **Wave 3 (about 20):** traits that touch other people (neighbours, the company family) and the three Scavenging ones, which need Scavenging first.

## Decided (2026-11-14)

- Traits are purely random: no companion has a trait that is always theirs, so some runs push a companion to retrain or push the player to hire someone else entirely.
- Three traits at 3% stays.
- The tavern shows a hire's traits before they are hired.
