# Elite fights, gemstones and accessories (design; built on 2026-11-14)

Every number here is a first guess, to be tuned with the 200-run check.

## Elite fights

- The last fight of weeks 1, 3 and 5 is an elite fight, always on the sixth day of the week: days 6, 20 and 34. The third battle of those weeks becomes the elite. In week 1 it moves from day 5 to day 6, so the company has two free days (4 and 5) to get ready for the first one.
- Two fronts to choose from, both at the highest danger. One must be fought.
- Won, it pays as a battle of that danger and gives a gemstone. Lost, it costs the walls as a lost front of that danger.

| Week | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | battle | free | battle | free | free | **elite** | free |
| 2 | battle | free | battle | free | battle | free | **boss** |
| 3 | free | battle | free | battle | free | **elite** | free |
| 4 | battle | free | battle | free | battle | free | **boss** |
| 5 | free | battle | free | battle | free | **elite** | free |
| 6 | battle | free | battle | free | battle | free | **boss** |

15 battles, 3 elites, 3 bosses, 21 free days.

## Gemstones

Five stones, weakest to strongest: **Topaz, Sapphire, Emerald, Ruby, Diamond** (ranks 1 to 5).

There are five big fights before the last boss, and each gives the next stone:

| Day | Fight | Stone |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | Elite | Topaz |
| 14 | Boss | Sapphire |
| 20 | Elite | Emerald |
| 28 | Boss | Ruby |
| 34 | Elite | Diamond |

- **Events:** a few event choices give a stone, usually at a price or a risk. It is the stone of the last big fight the company has passed (Topaz before day 6), or one rank lower.
- **Scavenging:** a Success has a very small chance (3%) to bring back a stone of that same rank.
- A run that wins everything sees about 6 or 7 stones. A full company has 10 to 12 places to wear them, so every accessory is a choice of who gets it.
- Stones are kept like timber and ore, and are worth nothing until the Jeweler sets them.

## The Jeweler

A tenth facility, paid in iron ore like the Smithy (3 for level 1, 5 for level 2).

| Level | What it does |
|---|---|
| 1 | Opens crafting |
| 2 | The jeweler shows two designs and the player picks one |

- **Crafting** is the day's action on a free day, and costs one stone and gold: 20 gold x the stone's rank (Topaz 20, Sapphire 40, Emerald 60, Ruby 80, Diamond 100).
- **What comes out is random:** one type drawn evenly from every type below. Its name is the stone and the type: "Topaz Ring", "Ruby Lantern".
- The stone decides how strong it is; the type decides what it does. An accessory is never re-made or upgraded.

## Wearing them

- Each companion wears up to two accessories, never two of the same type (not a Topaz Ring and a Ruby Ring).
- Put on, taken off and handed over at the Barracks, freely, like weapons: not once a front is committed.
- Whatever it gives to a stat is in the stats everywhere (the barracks, the Attack, weight, scavenging, the battle).
- Someone in the Infirmary keeps theirs on; it can be taken from them.

## Notes on balance

- A Diamond Ring is +5 Strength, about two and a half weapon tiers of Might. It comes on day 34 with eight days and one boss left, so it is a reward for the hardest elite and not something a run is built around.
- A Topaz of a "road" type (Acorn, Plume, Tassel) is worth most early; a Topaz Ring is worth little by week 4. That is the trade the random draw sets up: an early stone made at once, or kept in the hope of a better use.
- The plain types make up a fifth of the draws. With the Jeweler's second level (pick one of two) the chance that neither design suits anyone in the company is small.
- Six types need new rules (Pin, Thorn, Talisman, Locket, Hourglass, and the "elite fight" half of Sigil). They can be left out of a first build without changing anything else.

## Changed after the first 200-run check (2026-11-14)

- Spur no longer makes a plain attack, Guard or Focus free: it only shortens actions with a recovery of 2 or more.
- Poultice is flat points of Health, not a share of the overnight heal (a Topaz was about +4% a night).
- Bell also gives Defence: drawing blows did nothing for the wearer on its own.
- Compass also raises the haul: one scavenging check was too little.

## Open questions

- Should a type be drawn at most once in a run (as traits are), or can the same type come up again? The rule against wearing two of one type suggests it can.
- Can an accessory be broken up to get the stone back, or sold? Not in this design.
- Does a companion who leaves the line for the Infirmary keep their accessories locked to them? This design lets them be taken off.
