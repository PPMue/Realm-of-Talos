# The city's upgrades and enchanting (design; to be built)

Decided with the user on 2026-11-14. Numbers marked "first guess" are to be tuned with the 200-run check.

## Timber builds, iron ore forges

- **Timber** is the city: every facility upgrade (but the Smithy's and the Gate's) and repairs.
- **Iron ore** is arms: enchanting, moving an enchantment, and the Smithy's and the Gate's upgrades.
- **Repairs** cost timber only: 3 timber for 20 city health (was 2 timber and 1 iron ore).
- **Swap** at the Mason's Yard: 2 of one material for 1 of the other, any number of times.
- Upgrading, enchanting, moving an enchantment and swapping are errands like buying and curing: they
  cost materials (and gold where said), never the day's action, on free days once the day's event is
  answered. An upgrade works from the moment it is paid for.
- Revised on 2026-11-14: the Smithy (buying, enchanting, moving an enchantment) and the Tavern (hiring)
  are open on battle and boss days too, until the company marches out; an enchanting offer still open
  then is kept as "keep". Curing, upgrades, swaps, rest, drills and scavenging stay on free days.

## Facility upgrades

Nine facilities, two levels each, level 1 before level 2. A run cannot afford all eighteen: the costs
should let a run that takes materials whenever they are offered buy about six to eight (first guess:
level 1 costs 4, level 2 costs 8, of the facility's material).

| Facility | Pays in | Level 1 | Level 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Watchtower | timber | The boss days and which boss comes are known | Tomorrow's fronts are known a day early |
| Smithy | iron ore | Opens enchanting | The smith offers two enchantments to choose from |
| Tavern | timber | One more hire offered each week | Hires cost 25 gold less |
| Inn | timber | Everyone heals 30% overnight instead of 20% | Every spell gets one use back each night |
| Infirmary | timber | Stays are 3 days instead of 4 | Cures cost a quarter less |
| Drill Yard | timber | Drills never come out poor | A third drill is posted each week |
| Barracks | timber | The reserve gets a quarter share of a battle's XP | The reserve heals fully overnight |
| Mason's Yard | timber | Repairs mend 30 instead of 20 | The walls hold 120 instead of 100 |
| Gate | iron ore | A third scavenging site each day | A lost front costs the walls 5 less |

Without the Watchtower's first level the boss days are not marked and the bosses are not named: the
day strip and the watch's report show only what the unimproved tower can see.

## Enchanting

At the Smithy, once its first upgrade is built. Revised on 2026-11-15: a weapon is plain or enchanted
once ("Iron Sword+"); there are no levels.

- Any weapon the company owns can be enchanted, in hand or in the armory.
- **The budget** is the net stat points an enchantment adds up to: wooden 1, bronze 2, iron 3, steel 4,
  silver 5, platinum 6; a rare weapon 4.
- **A roll**: every point is +1 to a stat drawn at random from all eleven (Health, Stamina, Strength,
  Magic, Dexterity, Speed, Defence, Resistance, Critical, Luck, Build); the same stat can come up again,
  some come out duds, and that is intended. A quarter of the rolls with a budget of 2 or more are
  two-edged: one stat takes -k (k from 1 to half the budget) and k more points are rolled elsewhere, so
  the net is still the budget. At most one stat is negative.
- **Each attempt** costs 1 iron ore and 20 gold x the tier (at least 1; a rare weapon 60 gold), the
  same every time. The smith offers a new roll (two with the Smithy's second upgrade) and the player
  takes one or keeps what the weapon has; the cost is spent either way. An enchanted weapon can be
  tried again any number of times.
- The weapon is named with a + ("Iron Sword+") and lists what it gives ("+2 Strength", "-1 Luck").
- **Moving an enchantment**: for 2 iron ore the smith moves a weapon's whole enchantment to a plain
  owned weapon of the same type with at least the same budget.
